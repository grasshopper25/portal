Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Grasshopper Career Consultant collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Grasshopper Career Consultant. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our admission counseling services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when requesting information regarding MBBS admissions and educational consultancy services.

Personal Identification

Name, date of birth, and demographic information.

Contact Information

Email address, mobile number, and residential address.

Academic Information

NEET scores, educational qualifications, and academic records required for admission guidance.

Financial Information

Basic financial information required for scholarship and education loan assistance.

We may also collect anonymous usage information through cookies, analytics tools, and website tracking technologies to improve website performance and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • ✓ Evaluate eligibility for MBBS admissions.
  • ✓ Recommend suitable private medical colleges.
  • ✓ Process college applications and admission support.
  • ✓ Assist with scholarships and education loan guidance.
  • ✓ Send counselling updates, notifications, and customer support.

4. How We Share Your Information

Your information is shared only when necessary, with your consent, or to comply with applicable legal requirements.

Educational Institutions

Information may be shared with partner universities and medical colleges for admission processing.

Financial Institutions

Banks and financial partners may receive relevant information for education loan processing.

Service Providers

Trusted third-party vendors help us provide our services. Our website infrastructure, technical maintenance, and digital security are managed in collaboration with Turain Software.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

  • ✓ Request access to your personal information.
  • ✓ Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • ✓ Request deletion of your personal data where legally applicable.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
